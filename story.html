Ocean roots. Mountain people now.

Carolyn is from the Virgin Islands. Bruce is from Long Island. Together they run Black Mountain Seafood, a small shop with one big idea: bring the freshest seafood they can to the mountains, and become part of how this town plans its weekly meals.

Carrying on a legacy.

December 2022

A seafood shop on State Street

Roger and Debra Queen of Fairview Seafood Company open a Black Mountain location in a small building at 213 E State St.

Week after week

Two regulars

Carolyn and Bruce stop in nearly every week. A bag of fresh fish becomes their special treat at the end of the work week.

Late 2025

Taking the keys

They buy the Black Mountain shop from the Queens. Carolyn heads home to the Virgin Islands, then works at Fairview Seafood to learn the trade.

March 6, 2026

Black Mountain Seafood opens

Same great product, a new name, and a promise to change as little as possible about what the town already loved.

Today

Open year-round

Fresh catch every Friday and Saturday, a weekly email for pre-orders, and a growing book of community recipes.

Healthy food, from healthy waters.

  • Sustainably caught

    Most of our seafood is caught sustainably in the Atlantic, with a few responsibly aquacultured outliers.

  • Healthy food systems

    We're passionate about good food that's good for people, and about knowing where it comes from.

  • Two days, max fresh

    Picked up late Thursday night, kept on ice, sold Friday and Saturday. That's it. That's the secret.

  • Community first

    Recipe help at the counter, a shared recipe book, fish parties, and a whole lot of love for our customers.
