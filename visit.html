Questions, answered.

How do I pre-order?
Join our weekly email. Every Wednesday afternoon we send the updated fish inventory. Just reply to that email with what you'd like, and pick it up Friday or Saturday.
What will be in the case this week?
It changes every week depending on what's fresh. The Wednesday email lists everything, and we post the case in our Instagram stories on Fridays and Saturdays.
Where does your seafood come from?
Most of it is caught sustainably in the Atlantic Ocean, with a few responsibly aquacultured exceptions. We pick it up from our supplier late Thursday night and keep it on ice until you take it home.
Do you sell out?
Yes, sometimes. Popular items can go fast, especially on Saturdays. Pre-ordering through the weekly email is the best way to make sure you get what you want.
Can you help me figure out what to cook?
Absolutely, it's one of our favorite parts of the job. Ask at the counter, browse our recipe box, or add your own favorite to our community recipe book.
Are you open year-round?
Yes. We plan to stay open all year, with the occasional weekend off for holidays. Any closures show up in the hours section near the top of this page and in our weekly email.
Can I call?
Yes. Call us at (828) 417-1057. If you prefer email, write to hello@blackmountainseafood.com.
I have another question.
Email us at hello@blackmountainseafood.com and we'll get back to you.
